## Understanding hypertension

Hypertension (high blood pressure) is a silent, chronic condition where persistently high force against artery walls strains the heart, vessels, brain, and kidneys. This sustained pressure is the primary driver of cardiovascular damage. Unmanaged, it drastically increases the risk of stroke and renal failure.

### The silent danger & causes

Blood pressure measures force when the heart pumps (Systolic) and rests (Diastolic). Consistent readings above 130/80 mmHg cause arteries to stiffen.

- **Hypertension–Kidney Axis:** High pressure damages kidney vessels; conversely, kidney issues raise pressure, a dangerous cycle.
- **Types:** Most cases are Primary (genetics/lifestyle); Secondary stems from causes like kidney disease or sleep apnea.
