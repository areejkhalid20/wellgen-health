## Allergy testing and treatment

## Understanding allergies

Allergies are an overreaction of your immune system to substances that are typically harmless, such as pollen, pet dander, or certain foods. This health issue is incredibly common: over 50 million people in the United States experience allergies every year, making them one of the most widespread chronic conditions. With its diverse plant life and fluctuating weather, Texas is a major hotspot for seasonal allergies, affecting millions of residents. Allergies can cause disruptive respiratory symptoms, skin rashes, or gastrointestinal issues, significantly impacting your quality of life if they are not properly addressed.

## Causes and triggers of allergies

Allergies are fundamentally triggered by substances called allergens. The most common allergens are found everywhere and include pollen, dust mites, mold spores, and pet dander. Allergies can also be triggered by specific items like insect stings or certain foods, such as peanuts, shellfish, and eggs. While genetics play a significant role, increasing the likelihood if one or both parents have allergies, environmental factors are also contributors. Exposure to pollutants or a history of specific viral infections may also contribute to developing allergic reactions.

## Diagnosing allergies

Diagnosing allergies accurately involves a combination of reviewing your medical history, conducting a physical examination, and utilizing precise diagnostic tests. At WellGen Health, we provide comprehensive allergy testing to pinpoint the specific allergens causing your symptoms. Our testing includes:

- **Skin Prick Tests:** To check for immediate allergic reactions to various substances.
- **Blood Tests:** To measure the levels of allergy-related antibodies (IgE) in your blood.
- **Patch Tests:** To identify delayed allergic reactions, often related to skin contact.
- Early diagnosis is essential for effective management, helping us prevent severe reactions and significantly improve your quality of life.

## Treating and managing allergies

Effective allergy treatment aims to proactively reduce symptoms and prevent severe reactions. Common, immediate treatments include medications such as antihistamines, decongestants, and nasal corticosteroids.

For long-term relief, we offer immunotherapy (allergy shots). Immunotherapy works by gradually desensitizing your immune system to specific allergens, providing a sustained reduction in symptoms. Additionally, simple lifestyle adjustments, like diligently avoiding known allergens and using high-quality air purifiers, can significantly help manage your day-to-day symptoms.

At WellGen Health, we create personalized allergy management plans carefully tailored to your specific needs, allergen profile, and lifestyle, ensuring the most effective path to lasting relief.
