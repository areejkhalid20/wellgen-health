## What is hormone replacement therapy?

Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) is a medically supervised treatment designed to restore and rebalance the body's natural hormones (like estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, and thyroid hormones). As hormones decline with age (menopause/andropause), symptoms like persistent fatigue, mood changes, and low libido arise, affecting overall vitality and long-term health.

### BHRT and personalized care

- **Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT):** We specialize in BHRT, using plant-derived hormones that are chemically identical to your body's own for safer, more precise restoration.
- **Customized Approach:** Treatment starts with comprehensive lab testing and symptom review. We design a personalized plan, determining appropriate hormones and dosages, using delivery methods like Topical Creams/Gels or Hormone Pellets for consistent delivery.
- **Benefits:** HRT, when professionally monitored, delivers increased energy, improved mood, restored libido, and clearer thinking, contributing to better weight management and bone health.
