## Understanding arthritis

Arthritis is a broad term for over 100 conditions causing pain, swelling, and stiffness in the joints. It is a leading cause of disability, demanding early detection and a personalized care plan for effective management and long-term joint wellness. Our integrated approach is designed to restore mobility and improve your overall quality of life.

### Major categories and symptoms

Arthritis involves inflammation and damage where bones meet, restricting movement. Common symptoms include joint pain, morning stiffness, swelling, and fatigue.

- **Inflammatory Arthritis (e.g., Rheumatoid Arthritis):** The immune system mistakenly attacks joint tissues, causing chronic inflammation.
- **Degenerative Arthritis (Osteoarthritis):** Results from the wear and tear of cartilage, often affecting weight-bearing joints (knees, hips).
- **Soft Tissue Conditions:** Affects supporting structures like tendons, ligaments, and bursae.
