## Understanding chronic pain

Chronic pain is a complex condition lasting three months or longer, sustained by a hypersensitive nervous system (central sensitization), which makes it physically exhausting and challenging. It continues even after initial injury healing, often without a clear ongoing cause.

At WellGen Health, we treat chronic pain as a condition in its own right, impacting movement, sleep, work, and overall quality of life.

### The nature of pain and its impact

Pain is intermittent or constant and fluctuates with stress. Common physical and neurological sources include:

- Arthritis/Joint Disorders and Spinal Disorders.
- Fibromyalgia and Neuropathic Pain (e.g., due to diabetes).
- **Mind-Body Connection:** Emotional distress (anxiety, depression) intensifies pain perception. This is why managing mental health is integral to physical relief and sustainable recovery.

### Integrated diagnosis and management

We use a comprehensive evaluation (Imaging, EMG, pain mapping, holistic assessment) to find the source. Our multidisciplinary team (pain specialists, physiotherapists, psychologists) creates a customized roadmap. Education is key: we empower you with self-management and CBT to retrain the brain's response, helping to break the cycle of pain and inactivity and restore functional independence, ensuring long-term well-being. Partner with us to restore comfort, mobility, and hope.
