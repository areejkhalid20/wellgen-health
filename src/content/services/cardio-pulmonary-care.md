    a: "Your heart and lungs are vital interconnected systems that sustain life by circulating oxygen-rich blood. The cardiovascular system circulates oxygen-rich blood throughout the body via arteries and veins, while the pulmonary system ensures oxygen enters the bloodstream and expels carbon dioxide through respiration. During inhalation, oxygen transfers into the blood through tiny air sacs called alveoli; this oxygen-rich blood goes to the left heart and is pumped body-wide. ## Understanding cardiovascular and pulmonary health

Your heart and lungs are vital, interconnected systems that sustain life by circulating oxygen-rich blood. When these systems are strong, your entire body thrives; disruption leads to decreased oxygen supply, fatigue, and risk of chronic issues like heart failure or COPD. Understanding this seamless partnership is the key to maintaining optimal vitality and longevity.
