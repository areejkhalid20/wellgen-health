## Chronic care management at WellGen

At WellGen Health, we redefine chronic illness management with a proactive, continuous, and patient-centered model. Chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, COPD, arthritis) demand ongoing monitoring and coordination. Our programs bridge the gap between appointments with structured, technology-enabled support.

### The WellGen Health continuous care philosophy

We anticipate problems, offering uninterrupted, evidence-based oversight to prevent complications.

- **24/7 Clinical Access:** Real-time guidance from professionals.
- **Personalized Care Coordination:** Seamless management across your provider network.
- **Medication Management:** Frequent reviews to prevent adverse reactions.
- **Monthly Follow-Ups:** Proactive check-ins by a dedicated Care Coordinator.

### CCM & PCM programs

- **CCM:** For patients managing two or more chronic conditions (e.g., Diabetes and Hypertension). Coordinated by a primary provider.
- **PCM:** For patients with a single, high-risk, or complex condition (e.g., Severe Heart Failure). Guided by a medical specialist.

Both programs use advanced monitoring to empower you, leading to fewer hospitalizations and a significant enhancement in quality of life.
