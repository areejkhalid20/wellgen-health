## The purpose of surgery clearance and risk assessment

The purpose of a surgical clearance and risk assessment is to safeguard your health and optimize surgical outcomes. While surgeons focus on the technical aspects of the operation, our job at WellGen Health is to ensure that your body is fully prepared to handle the stress of surgery.

This evaluation helps to:

- Detect underlying health issues that may complicate anesthesia or the procedure itself.
- Assess cardiovascular, pulmonary, and metabolic fitness to determine your surgical tolerance.
- Optimize chronic conditions, such as high blood pressure, diabetes, or heart disease, before your operation.
- Collaborate with your surgical team to design a coordinated, safe, and efficient care plan.

With these goals in mind, every patient receives an individualized assessment tailored to their specific surgery type, medical background, and recovery needs.
