## What is geriatric care?

Geriatric Care is specialized medicine for older adults, focusing on promoting health, preventing disease, and enhancing the quality of life. Aging involves complex physical and cognitive changes requiring a comprehensive approach.

### A holistic approach to aging

At WellGen Health, our Geriatric Care and Age Management programs look at the whole person, addressing interconnected conditions, medications, mental health, and lifestyle.

Core principles include:

- Prevention & Early Detection.
- **Medication Management:** Critical review to minimize side effects.
- **Functional Independence:** Promoting strength and balance.
- Cognitive & Emotional Support.

### Promoting healthy aging

Geriatric care helps people age with vitality and dignity. Our programs integrate preventive medicine, physiotherapy, and cognitive training to help seniors maintain:

- Hormonal & Metabolic Balance.
- Chronic Disease Management.
- Independence.

By blending expertise with personalized wellness, WellGen Health ensures aging is a period of continued strength.
