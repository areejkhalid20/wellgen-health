## Comprehensive personal injury evaluations

After an accident (car, workplace, slip and fall), a Comprehensive Personal Injury Evaluation is the vital first step. WellGen Health delivers an in-depth medical assessment to identify the true extent of your injuries, ensure precision documentation, and set the foundation for recovery.

### Our proven evaluation process

We use a meticulous process to uncover visible and hidden injuries (whiplash, soft tissue damage) and provide objective evidence:

- **History & Physical Exam:** Detailed review of the mechanism of injury and extensive check of Range of Motion and neurological function.
- **Advanced Diagnostics:** We utilize Digital X-rays, MRI/CT Scans (for soft tissue/spinal injuries), and Electrodiagnostic Tests (EMG/NCS) to evaluate nerve function.
- **Functional Assessment:** Records pain levels and assesses emotional impacts and effect on daily activities.
- **Documentation:** Detailed reports provide essential, objective evidence for insurance and legal claims.

### Why precision evaluation matters

Early, thorough evaluation is critical to preventing complications and permanent damage. We provide a roadmap for healing, coordinating seamlessly with all specialists to eliminate delays and speed up recovery. Our unbiased, evidence-based reports are trusted by legal and insurance.
