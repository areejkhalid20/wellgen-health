## Understanding sleep disorders

Sleep is vital for health, tissue repair, and hormone regulation. A sleep disorder interferes with achieving quality, restful sleep, leading to daytime fatigue and increased risk of chronic diseases (hypertension, diabetes, cardiovascular disease).

We know sleep is a biological necessity. We provide science-based care to uncover the underlying causes

### Key disorders & impact

Sleep disorders are medical conditions preventing restorative rest. Common types include:

- **Insomnia:** Difficulty falling/staying asleep, often linked to stress.
- **Sleep Apnea:** Breathing stops repeatedly during sleep (snoring/choking), causing fragmented rest.
- **Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS):** Uncontrollable urge to move legs at night.

Disrupted sleep impairs memory consolidation, immunity, and mood. Warning signs include excessive daytime fatigue, loud snoring, and poor focus.

Our approach targets root causes, assessing both Circadian Rhythm (internal clock) and behavioral factors.
