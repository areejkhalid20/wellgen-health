## Understanding diabetes

Diabetes is a long-term condition where your body can't use insulin properly to turn food into energy. Sugar builds up in your blood, and over time it can affect your heart, kidneys, eyes and nerves. Understanding your condition is the first step toward managing it well.

### Types and symptoms

- **Type 1:** an autoimmune condition that needs lifelong insulin.
- **Type 2:** the most common type, caused by insulin resistance linked to lifestyle and genetics.
- **Gestational:** develops during pregnancy.

Common signs include increased thirst, frequent urination and tiredness. We offer A1C and other screenings for early detection, and help people with prediabetes prevent or delay type 2 diabetes.

### Care from one connected team

Our programs bring together endocrinologists, dietitians and diabetes educators. Your care can include:

- An individual treatment plan, including continuous glucose monitoring and insulin pump therapy when needed
- Nutrition counseling and exercise guidance
- Support for stress and overall wellbeing

## Causes and risk factors

Diabetes comes from a mix of genetic, hormonal, environmental and lifestyle factors. Knowing your risks lets you take steps early.

- **Family history:** genes raise your risk, especially for type 2. We offer early screening and prevention.
- **Excess weight:** fat around the waist causes insulin resistance. We build weight management plans with you.
- **Low activity:** without regular exercise, your body uses sugar less well. Activity is part of every plan.
- **Diet:** meals high in refined foods cause blood sugar spikes. Our nutrition team helps you eat for steadier levels.
- **Other factors:** hormone conditions like PCOS, high stress, poor sleep, age and ethnicity can also raise your risk.

## Diagnosing diabetes

We use a full set of tests to confirm a diagnosis and understand what is driving it.

| Test | What it shows | Points to diabetes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fasting blood glucose | Your blood sugar after not eating overnight | 126 mg/dL or higher |
| A1C | Your average blood sugar over 2 to 3 months | 6.5% or higher |
| Oral glucose tolerance test | How your body handles sugar over two hours | Used for gestational diabetes and prediabetes |
| Continuous glucose monitoring | Your blood sugar day and night, in real time | Shows patterns that guide treatment |
| Hormone panels | Thyroid, cortisol and C-peptide levels | Gives a full picture of your metabolism |

Your results become the starting point of your treatment plan, and we explain each one so you know what it means.

## Managing diabetes

Managing diabetes is a long-term partnership. The goal is steady blood sugar, fewer complications and a better quality of life.

### Treatment matched to your results

Our providers use your test results and glucose monitoring data to adjust insulin and other medications precisely. Your plan also covers:

- **Medical nutrition therapy:** a personal eating plan for steadier blood sugar.
- **Exercise:** activity that improves insulin sensitivity and heart health.
- **Prevention:** regular checks for heart disease, nerve damage and other complications.

### Support beyond medication

We look after your whole hormone system, including thyroid and PCOS. You also get education and mental health support, so managing diabetes becomes part of daily life rather than a burden.

## Take charge of your health

Your care starts with a full assessment of your hormones and blood sugar to find the cause of your symptoms. From there, we build a plan that brings together medication, nutrition and lifestyle changes, and we stay with you through regular follow-ups and monitoring.
