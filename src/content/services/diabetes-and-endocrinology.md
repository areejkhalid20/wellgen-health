## Diabetes and hormone care in one place

Diabetes is a long-term condition where your body can't use insulin properly, so sugar builds up in your blood. Over time it can affect your heart, kidneys, eyes and nerves. Because diabetes is closely linked to your hormones, we look after the whole hormone system, including thyroid, adrenal and reproductive hormones such as PCOS.

Your care is led by endocrinologists working with dietitians and diabetes educators. Together they find what is driving your blood sugar, build a plan around your results and your life, and adjust it with you over time.
