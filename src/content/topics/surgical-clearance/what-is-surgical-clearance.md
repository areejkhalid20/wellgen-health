This critical step helps protect your health, minimize risks, and promote the best possible surgical outcomes.

At WellGen Health, surgical clearance is much more than a basic check-up. Our experienced clinicians perform a complete evaluation of your overall health, carefully reviewing every detail of your medical history, medications, allergies, and any underlying conditions that could influence your surgical experience or healing process. By identifying and managing potential health risks early, we help ensure that your surgery is performed safely, efficiently, and with confidence.

## The purpose of surgical clearance

The goal of surgical clearance is to create a clear, evidence-based picture of your current health. Surgeons and anesthesiologists rely on this information to determine whether any pre-existing conditions, medications, or physiological factors could pose complications during surgery.

At WellGen Health, we focus on three core objectives during your preoperative assessment:

- Identify and manage existing medical conditions that could interfere with anesthesia, wound healing, or recovery.
- Reduce the likelihood of surgical complications, including bleeding, infection, or cardiovascular stress.
- Optimize your overall health so that you can experience the smoothest possible surgery and recovery process.

This careful, proactive approach allows us to safeguard your health and provide your surgical team with the vital information they need to make informed decisions about your care.

## Why surgical clearance is essential

Surgical clearance plays a crucial role in preventing complications and ensuring your body is prepared for the physical demands of surgery. Even seemingly minor procedures can place temporary stress on the cardiovascular, respiratory, and immune systems.

For instance, patients with hypertension, diabetes, heart disease, or respiratory disorders may face elevated surgical risks if these conditions are not properly controlled beforehand. Through comprehensive evaluation, our specialists can:

- Adjust medications to stabilize your condition.
- Recommend pre-surgical treatments or interventions.
- Coordinate care with your surgeon and any necessary specialists.

By addressing these details early, we not only protect your safety during surgery but also enhance your recovery afterward.

## Comprehensive evaluation before surgery

Your surgical clearance appointment at WellGen Health typically includes the following key components:

- In-depth Medical History Review – We analyze your personal and family medical history, including prior surgeries, chronic conditions, and any recent health changes.
- Physical Examination – A thorough physical exam is performed to assess your heart, lungs, blood pressure, weight, and overall physical condition.
- Diagnostic Testing – Depending on your age, surgery type, and health profile, we may order tests such as:
- Complete blood count (CBC) and metabolic panel
- Electrocardiogram (EKG/ECG)
- Chest X-ray
- Urinalysis
- Coagulation studies or other specialized evaluations
- Risk Assessment – Our team identifies potential risk factors and develops strategies to manage them effectively.
- Coordination with Your Surgeon and Anesthesiologist – We communicate directly with your surgical team to ensure everyone involved has access to your clearance results and recommendations.

This thorough, data-driven process ensures accuracy and transparency; two pillars that define WellGen Health's standard of care.

## The role of communication and collaboration

One of the most important aspects of surgical clearance is collaboration. At WellGen Health, we work closely with your surgeon, anesthesiologist, and any specialists managing your chronic conditions (such as cardiologists, endocrinologists, or pulmonologists).

Our goal is to streamline the preoperative process by providing clear, timely reports and actionable recommendations. This coordination minimizes delays, ensures compliance with hospital and surgical center requirements, and provides your care team with the confidence to proceed safely.

## Who needs surgical clearance?

Surgical clearance is typically required for patients undergoing:

- Major or elective surgeries (orthopedic, cardiac, abdominal, etc.)
- Outpatient or same-day procedures requiring anesthesia
- Cosmetic or reconstructive surgeries
- Dental or oral surgeries involving sedation
- Emergency procedures (when time permits)

However, even if your procedure seems minor, obtaining clearance offers peace of mind that your body is ready to handle anesthesia, surgical stress, and recovery.

Patients over the age of 40 or those with chronic health conditions particularly benefit from a detailed preoperative assessment.
