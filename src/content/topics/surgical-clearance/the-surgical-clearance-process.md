Surgery, no matter how minor or major, places stress on the body. By completing a thorough preoperative evaluation, our clinicians can identify potential health risks, correct underlying issues, and prepare you both physically and mentally for your upcoming procedure. The result is improved safety, fewer complications, and greater peace of mind for both patients and surgical teams.

## Step 1: comprehensive medical history review

Your surgical clearance begins with a detailed review of your personal and family medical history. This foundational step allows our clinicians to gain a complete understanding of your overall health and identify any conditions that might affect anesthesia or surgery.

During this consultation, we evaluate:

- Chronic conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, asthma, or heart disease.
- Past surgeries and how you responded to anesthesia in previous procedures.
- Allergies or medication sensitivities, particularly to anesthetics, antibiotics, or pain medications.
- Current medications and supplements, including over-the-counter drugs or herbal remedies that could interfere with surgical outcomes.
- Family medical history, which can reveal genetic factors like clotting disorders or cardiovascular risks.

This step establishes the foundation of your surgical risk profile. It allows us to anticipate possible complications, adjust your care plan, and coordinate seamlessly with your surgical team.

## Step 2: in-depth physical examination

Once your health history is reviewed, we perform a comprehensive physical examination to evaluate how your body is functioning. This includes an assessment of vital systems essential to surgical and anesthetic safety.

Your exam will typically include:

- Cardiovascular evaluation; checking heart rate, rhythm, and blood pressure to assess cardiac health.
- Respiratory assessment; evaluating lung function, oxygen levels, and breathing capacity, especially for patients with asthma, COPD, or sleep apnea.
- Neurological evaluation; assessing reflexes, coordination, and cognitive function to establish a pre-surgical baseline.
- General health assessment; including weight, temperature, and any signs of infection, inflammation, or weakness.

This hands-on assessment gives our team a clear picture of your physical readiness and helps determine whether additional testing or specialist consultation is required before surgery.

## Step 3: diagnostic and laboratory testing

Common tests may include:

- **Blood Work:** Complete blood count (CBC), metabolic panel, and clotting tests to evaluate organ function, blood health, and immune readiness.
- **Electrocardiogram (EKG or ECG):** To assess heart rhythm and electrical activity, identifying potential cardiac concerns that may require management before anesthesia.
- **Chest X-rays or Imaging Scans:** Particularly for patients with respiratory issues or those undergoing chest or abdominal procedures.
- **Urinalysis:** To detect infections, kidney issues, or metabolic imbalances that could impact recovery.
- **Blood Sugar and Lipid Panels:** Especially for patients with diabetes or metabolic disorders, ensuring blood glucose levels are stable before surgery.
- **Additional Specialty Tests:** Depending on your medical background, we may recommend pulmonary function tests, stress testing, or echocardiography to provide a more complete picture of your health.

These diagnostic tools are essential for uncovering hidden conditions that may increase surgical risks. Once your results are reviewed, our clinicians interpret the data in context; ensuring recommendations are precise, evidence-based, and individualized.

## Step 4: risk assessment and health optimization

Following diagnostic testing, our team performs a comprehensive surgical risk assessment to evaluate how your body may respond to surgery and anesthesia.

This step involves:

- Identifying red flags such as uncontrolled blood pressure, irregular heart rhythms, or respiratory instability.
- Classifying risk level according to standardized medical scales (such as ASA physical status classification) used by anesthesiologists to guide surgical decisions.
- Creating a proactive optimization plan to manage any issues before surgery.

If we detect areas of concern, WellGen Health takes immediate steps to stabilize your condition. This may include:

- Adjusting medications to balance blood pressure or glucose levels.
- Recommending nutritional improvements to support healing and immune function.
- Encouraging light physical activity or pulmonary exercises to strengthen cardiovascular and respiratory endurance.
- Coordinating additional evaluations with cardiologists, endocrinologists, or pulmonologists if specialized care is required.

## Step 5: communication and collaboration with your surgical team

Surgical clearance is a team effort; and communication is the key to ensuring the best outcomes. Once your evaluation is complete, WellGen Health sends a detailed medical report directly to your surgeon and anesthesiologist.

This report includes:

- All relevant test results and clinical findings.
- A clear summary of your overall health status and risk level.
- Personalized recommendations for perioperative care, including medication management or follow-up requirements.

Our team maintains open lines of communication with your healthcare providers throughout the process. If your surgeon requests additional information or follow-up tests, we respond promptly to keep your surgery on schedule.

## Step 6: final clearance and preoperative approval

Once all evaluations and necessary optimizations are complete, you will receive your final surgical clearance from WellGen Health. This clearance indicates that you are medically fit for anesthesia and your scheduled procedure.

Before final approval, our clinicians review all data to confirm:

- Vital signs and lab results are stable and within safe ranges.
- Any chronic conditions are well-controlled.
- All preoperative instructions have been clearly communicated and understood.

You'll also receive personalized guidance on what to do in the days leading up to surgery, such as fasting instructions, medication adjustments, and pre-surgery hygiene recommendations. Our goal is to ensure you feel informed, prepared, and confident as you approach your procedure.
