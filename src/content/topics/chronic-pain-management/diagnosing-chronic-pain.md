Chronic pain is often complex and multifactorial; sometimes arising from more than one condition. That's why our diagnostic approach combines advanced medical imaging, neurological evaluations, functional assessments, and emotional health screenings. By integrating the latest technology with clinical expertise, we uncover the underlying mechanisms driving your pain; whether they stem from nerve injury, musculoskeletal dysfunction, inflammation, or systemic disease.

Our multidisciplinary pain specialists work collaboratively to ensure every evaluation is comprehensive, compassionate, and tailored to you. We take the time to listen, understand your history, and interpret how your pain affects your movement, mood, and lifestyle. This whole-person approach ensures that every treatment plan is grounded in accuracy, safety, and lasting effectiveness.

## A precision-based approach to pain diagnosis

Pain is a personal experience; no two cases are ever the same. That's why WellGen Health uses precision diagnostics to determine where the pain originates, why it persists, and how it impacts your daily life.

Our diagnostic philosophy is built around three essential principles:

- **Identify the Physical Source of Pain:** Using advanced imaging and testing to locate structural or neurological abnormalities.
- **Understand the Functional Impact:** Assessing how pain limits mobility, strength, and overall performance.
- **Recognize the Emotional and Behavioral Dimensions:** Exploring how pain affects sleep, stress levels, mood, and mental health.

This multi-dimensional evaluation allows us to design care strategies that treat the cause, not just the symptoms.

## Comprehensive diagnostic tools at WellGen Health

Our clinics are equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic technologies that provide detailed insights into the body's structure and function. Each test is selected based on your symptoms, history, and suspected pain origin.

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) and Computed Tomography (CT) Scans

MRI and CT scans are critical tools for visualizing soft tissue, bones, joints, and nerves.

- **MRI Scans:** Offer highly detailed images of muscles, ligaments, discs, and nerves; helping to detect issues like herniated discs, arthritis, inflammation, or soft tissue tears.
- **CT Scans:** Provide cross-sectional imaging ideal for identifying fractures, bone degeneration, or structural misalignments.

These imaging studies help pinpoint physical abnormalities that may be triggering chronic pain, guiding our specialists in formulating a precise treatment plan.

## Electromyography (EMG) and nerve conduction studies (NCS)

When pain involves numbness, tingling, or weakness, EMG and NCS are performed to measure how nerves and muscles communicate.

- EMG evaluates the electrical activity of muscles.
- NCS measures the speed and strength of nerve signals.

Together, they help diagnose neuropathic pain conditions such as peripheral neuropathy, sciatica, carpal tunnel syndrome, or post-surgical nerve damage.

## Diagnostic injections and pain mapping

Pain mapping is an advanced diagnostic method where targeted injections are used to isolate the specific pain generator.

- Nerve Blocks and Joint Injections help determine if a particular nerve or joint is the pain source.
- Facet and Epidural Injections may confirm spinal pain origins.This technique not only identifies the cause of pain but can also provide temporary relief, validating the next steps in treatment planning.

## Functional and mobility assessments

Pain doesn't just exist; it affects how you move, sit, and function. Our functional evaluations measure range of motion, muscle strength, posture, gait, and flexibility. Using motion analysis and physical performance testing, our therapists identify movement patterns that may worsen pain or limit mobility.

These assessments form a vital component of our rehabilitation strategy, ensuring that treatment supports both healing and physical reconditioning.

## Beyond the physical: evaluating the emotional and cognitive impact of pain

Chronic pain is not only a physical sensation; it has deep psychological and emotional effects. Prolonged pain can alter brain chemistry, disrupt sleep, and heighten stress or anxiety. At WellGen Health, we recognize this mind-body connection as an essential part of diagnosis.

Our comprehensive evaluations include:

- **Mental Health Screening:** Assessing mood, anxiety, depression, and coping strategies.
- **Sleep Quality Assessments:** Identifying sleep disturbances that may worsen pain perception.
- **Stress and Cognitive Evaluations:** Measuring concentration, fatigue, and emotional resilience.

This integrated perspective helps us understand how chronic pain affects every aspect of life; enabling us to deliver care that addresses both the body and the mind.

## The diagnostic process: what to expect at WellGen Health

Your journey at WellGen Health begins with a detailed consultation and medical history review. We discuss when and how the pain started, what worsens or relieves it, and how it influences your day-to-day routine.

Step 1: Comprehensive History and Symptom ReviewWe explore previous injuries, surgeries, and medical conditions while documenting your pain pattern; its intensity, frequency, and triggers.

Step 2: Physical ExaminationOur clinicians perform a detailed musculoskeletal and neurological assessment, checking posture, flexibility, reflexes, and muscle strength.

Step 3: Diagnostic TestingBased on initial findings, we may recommend MRI, CT, EMG, or diagnostic injections to visualize and confirm pain sources.

Step 4: Functional and Lifestyle EvaluationWe assess mobility, activity levels, and ergonomic habits. Lifestyle factors like sleep, nutrition, and stress are reviewed to identify contributors to pain persistence.

Step 5: Emotional and Cognitive ScreeningIf needed, our behavioral specialists conduct brief mental health evaluations to understand how chronic pain is affecting mood and coping capacity.

Once all data is gathered, our team collaborates to interpret findings and design a personalized care roadmap that blends medical treatment, physical rehabilitation, and emotional support.

## Identifying pain types for targeted treatment

Not all pain is alike. Proper diagnosis requires distinguishing between different categories of pain, as each demands a unique treatment approach.

- **Musculoskeletal Pain:** Originates from muscles, bones, or joints; often due to strain, arthritis, or injury.
- **Neuropathic Pain:** Results from nerve injury or dysfunction, producing burning or tingling sensations.
- **Inflammatory Pain:** Caused by immune responses or chronic inflammation in tissues.
- **Centralized Pain:** Occurs when the nervous system amplifies pain signals even without physical damage.

At WellGen Health, our diagnostic model helps classify pain precisely; allowing for customized interventions such as physical therapy, regenerative medicine, or nerve modulation therapy.
