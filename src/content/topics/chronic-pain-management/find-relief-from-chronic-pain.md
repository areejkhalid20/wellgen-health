## Non-surgical and minimally invasive options

For many patients, chronic pain can be managed effectively without the need for surgery. Our specialists at WellGen Health offer a wide range of non-surgical and minimally invasive pain relief options that target the source of discomfort while minimizing downtime and recovery periods.

Common procedures include:

- Epidural Steroid Injections to calm inflammation around spinal nerves.
- Nerve Blocks to temporarily interrupt pain signals from affected regions.
- Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA) to disable overactive pain nerves for months or years of relief.
- Trigger Point and Joint Injections to ease muscle tension and restore joint mobility.
- Spinal Cord Stimulation (SCS) for patients with severe or neuropathic pain unresponsive to conventional treatments.

These techniques are guided by advanced imaging for accuracy and safety, allowing patients to experience relief quickly and resume normal activities with confidence.
