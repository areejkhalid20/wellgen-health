Diagnosing sleep disorders requires a structured, evidence-based process that combines medical expertise, sleep monitoring technology, and a patient-centered approach. At WellGen Health, we follow a comprehensive diagnostic pathway designed to uncover the root of your sleep difficulties and guide you toward the most effective treatment.

## Comprehensive clinical evaluation

Your diagnostic journey begins with an in-depth clinical evaluation conducted by our sleep medicine specialists. This stage establishes the foundation for accurate diagnosis and personalized treatment planning. During your visit, our team takes the time to understand your unique sleep patterns, medical history, and lifestyle habits that may influence your sleep health.

- Sleep History and Patterns

We begin by discussing your typical bedtime and wake time, frequency of nighttime awakenings, daytime naps, and overall sleep quality. These details reveal essential clues about your sleep–wake cycle, insomnia triggers, or irregular sleep patterns. Our specialists also explore environmental factors; such as light exposure, noise levels, and screen use; that could interfere with healthy sleep.

- Daytime Symptoms and Functional Impact

The next step is identifying how your sleep problems affect your daytime life. Symptoms like fatigue, daytime drowsiness, lack of focus, low motivation, or mood changes are hallmark indicators of disrupted sleep. Understanding these consequences helps us determine the severity and potential type of disorder; whether it's sleep apnea, hypersomnia, circadian rhythm disruption, or another condition.

- Medical and Lifestyle Review

Sleep is closely tied to your overall health. Our evaluation includes a detailed review of medical conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, thyroid imbalance, and cardiovascular disease, which can contribute to sleep issues. We also examine medication use; since certain antidepressants, antihistamines, and stimulants can alter sleep patterns; and lifestyle factors like diet, caffeine or alcohol intake, shift work, and stress. This comprehensive review ensures that all potential contributing elements are addressed.

- Physical Examination

Our clinicians perform a physical exam focused on identifying anatomical and physiological indicators that may interfere with sleep. We assess your airway structure (including tonsils, nasal passages, and jaw alignment), measure your body mass index (BMI) and neck circumference, and check for signs of movement disorders or neurological abnormalities. These observations provide valuable insights into conditions such as obstructive sleep apnea, restless legs syndrome, or other movement-related sleep disturbances.

Through this detailed evaluation, we establish a clear baseline understanding of your sleep challenges, allowing us to recommend the most appropriate diagnostic tests with precision.

## Sleep monitoring & diagnostic studies

Once your clinical assessment is complete, the next step involves objective sleep monitoring. These studies help us visualize your sleep stages, detect breathing irregularities, and capture biological signals that reveal underlying disorders. Depending on your symptoms, our specialists may recommend one or more of the following diagnostic tools.

- Sleep Diary or Sleep Log

You may be asked to keep a sleep diary over one to two weeks. In it, you'll record your bedtime, wake time, nighttime awakenings, nap duration, and perceived sleep quality. This self-reporting tool helps identify patterns and triggers that contribute to insomnia, delayed sleep phase, or poor sleep hygiene.

- Actigraphy

For certain cases, we use actigraphy; a wrist-worn device that continuously tracks movement and light exposure. This technology provides detailed insights into your circadian rhythm, sleep duration, and rest–activity cycles over several days or weeks. It is particularly useful for diagnosing circadian rhythm sleep disorders and evaluating the impact of shift work.

- Home Sleep Testing (HST)

If obstructive sleep apnea is suspected, a convenient home sleep test may be recommended. This portable device records your breathing patterns, airflow, oxygen levels, heart rate, and body position overnight. Home testing allows for accurate detection of sleep apnea in a familiar environment and is often sufficient for straightforward cases.

- Overnight Polysomnography (PSG)

For complex or inconclusive cases, we conduct an in-lab sleep study known as polysomnography; the gold standard for diagnosing sleep disorders. During this overnight test, sensors record your brain waves, eye movements, muscle activity, heart rhythm, breathing patterns, oxygen saturation, and body movements. The resulting data offers a comprehensive picture of your sleep architecture, helping identify conditions such as sleep apnea, parasomnias, narcolepsy, and periodic limb movement disorder.

- Specialized Daytime Tests

In cases of excessive daytime sleepiness or suspected narcolepsy, specialized tests such as the Multiple Sleep Latency Test (MSLT) or the Maintenance of Wakefulness Test (MWT) are conducted. These evaluate how quickly you fall asleep in quiet situations or how well you can stay awake, offering insights into hypersomnia and alertness disorders.

These diagnostic studies give us an objective look at your sleep quality, breathing stability, and neurological patterns. Together with your clinical data, they enable us to pinpoint the exact cause of your sleep disturbance and guide your personalized treatment plan.

## Tailored diagnostic report & personalized treatment plan

After all evaluations and tests are complete, our sleep specialists compile a detailed diagnostic report. This report is more than a set of numbers; it's a roadmap to understanding your sleep health.

Your diagnostic summary includes:

- Personalized treatment options – Based on your diagnosis, we design a tailored management plan. This may include positive airway pressure therapy (CPAP or BiPAP) for sleep apnea, cognitive-behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I), chronotherapy for circadian rhythm issues, or medication and lifestyle modification for restless legs syndrome and other disorders.
- Education and empowerment – Our specialists explain every aspect of your diagnosis and treatment recommendations in clear, understandable terms. We ensure that you know what each test result means and how your treatment will improve your sleep quality and overall health.
- Follow-up and monitoring – Sleep health is a dynamic process. We schedule follow-up assessments to track progress, adjust treatments as needed, and ensure lasting improvement in sleep quality and daytime functioning.

At WellGen Health, our goal is not just to diagnose your sleep disorder but to empower you with knowledge and long-term solutions. Our team believes that when patients understand their condition, they can take an active role in their recovery and maintain healthier sleep habits.

## The importance of accurate diagnosis

Identifying the root cause of sleep disturbances offers more than short-term relief; it improves long-term health outcomes. Untreated sleep disorders can contribute to high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, depression, and reduced cognitive performance. By investing in accurate diagnosis, you take the most important step toward preventing these complications and restoring optimal health.

Each diagnostic stage at WellGen Health; clinical evaluation, sleep monitoring, and personalized reporting; is designed to work in harmony. Together, they transform uncertainty into clarity and confusion into a clear, actionable plan for better sleep.
