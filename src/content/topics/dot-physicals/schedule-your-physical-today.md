## What you can expect at your appointment

When you schedule your Pre-employment or DOT Physical with WellGen Health, you can expect a streamlined and transparent process designed around your needs:

- Physical ExaminationWe perform a thorough evaluation of your cardiovascular, musculoskeletal, and neurological systems, checking vital signs, strength, flexibility, and reflexes.
- Vision and Hearing ScreeningClear sensory performance is crucial for safety-sensitive occupations. We verify your compliance with the FMCSA and employer-specific vision and hearing standards.
- Urinalysis and Lab TestingA quick and non-invasive urine test helps detect underlying conditions such as diabetes, kidney issues, or high blood pressure that could impact work performance.
- Documentation and CertificationOnce your physical is complete and you meet all requirements, we provide immediate documentation. Commercial drivers receive their Medical Examiner's Certificate (MEC), typically valid for up to two years.

Our electronic record system ensures that your employer or licensing authority receives official documentation promptly, keeping you compliant and ready to work without unnecessary delays.

## Stay compliant and road-ready

For commercial drivers, staying compliant with FMCSA medical certification standards is not optional; it's the law. Every driver operating a commercial motor vehicle (CMV) must pass a DOT physical exam conducted by a certified examiner listed on the National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners (NRCME).

At WellGen Health, our certified providers are recognized by the FMCSA, ensuring that every DOT physical you complete meets federal regulations. If you have a chronic medical condition; such as hypertension, diabetes, or sleep apnea; we work closely with you to monitor and manage your health while maintaining your certification status.

Our proactive approach ensures that you remain safe, compliant, and confident on the road.
