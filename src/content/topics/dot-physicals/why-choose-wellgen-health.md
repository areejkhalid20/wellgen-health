We know that convenience is key, especially when balancing busy work schedules and compliance deadlines. That's why WellGen Health offers a full spectrum of occupational health services in one location; saving you time and simplifying the process. Our services include:

- Pre-employment physicals tailored to job-specific requirements
- DOT and CDL physical exams for commercial drivers
- Drug and alcohol testing for compliance and workplace safety
- Vision and hearing screening for sensory qualification
- Functional capacity and fitness-for-duty evaluations
- Immunizations, lab testing, and wellness exams

## Fast, reliable, and accurate results

In the fast-paced world of employment and transportation, time is valuable. WellGen Health prioritizes speed without sacrificing quality or compliance. Our streamlined processes, electronic documentation systems, and on-site lab services allow us to deliver results within hours, not days.
