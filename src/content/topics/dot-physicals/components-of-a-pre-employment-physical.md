## Core components of a pre-employment physical at WellGen Health

While every evaluation is customized, most pre-employment physicals include the following components designed to ensure overall fitness, safety, and readiness for work.

- Comprehensive Medical History Review

Your medical history is a key foundation of your pre-employment physical. During this step, our healthcare providers review your personal and family health background, including:

- Previous illnesses, surgeries, or injuries that may impact your ability to perform job tasks
- Current or chronic medical conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, or asthma
- Medications you are currently taking
- Allergies or past reactions to medications or workplace exposures

This information helps our team identify potential health concerns or risks that could interfere with job performance. It also allows us to make personalized recommendations for long-term wellness and workplace safety.

- Full Physical Examination

A comprehensive physical exam evaluates your overall health and functional capacity. Our clinicians perform a head-to-toe assessment that typically includes:

- **Vital Signs:** Blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, and oxygen saturation levels
- **Cardiovascular Health:** Assessment of heart rhythm, blood circulation, and endurance capacity
- **Respiratory Function:** Lung capacity, breathing patterns, and any signs of respiratory distress
- **Musculoskeletal Evaluation:** Flexibility, balance, posture, and joint mobility
- **Neurological Screening:** Reflexes, coordination, and motor control

This full-body evaluation ensures that you are physically prepared to handle the job's daily demands safely and effectively.

- Vision and Hearing Tests

Many workplaces, especially those involving machinery operation, driving, or public safety, require clear vision and hearing for safe performance. Our vision testing measures visual acuity, depth perception, peripheral vision, and color recognition to ensure you meet employer or federal standards.

The hearing assessment checks your ability to detect normal conversation and sound frequencies, helping identify potential auditory issues that could affect communication or job safety. These sensory evaluations are conducted using calibrated, industry-standard equipment for precise results.

- Drug and Alcohol Screening

Maintaining a safe, drug-free workplace is a top priority for most employers. At WellGen Health, we offer confidential and federally compliant drug and alcohol testing as part of our pre-employment physicals.

Our clinic uses certified laboratory testing and rapid screening options to detect commonly used substances, including:

- Amphetamines
- Opiates
- Marijuana (THC)
- Cocaine
- Phencyclidine (PCP)
- Alcohol (via breath or urine testing)

All tests are performed discreetly and securely, ensuring accurate results while protecting your privacy. Employers receive verified reports promptly to minimize hiring delays.

- Functional and Job-Specific Assessments

In physically demanding roles, general fitness may not be enough; job-specific assessments help confirm that you can perform essential duties safely. These functional capacity tests may include:

- **Lifting or Carrying Tests:** Measuring your ability to lift and move objects safely within workplace weight limits.
- **Endurance and Stamina Evaluations:** Assessing cardiovascular and muscular endurance.
- **Range of Motion and Flexibility Tests:** Ensuring proper joint movement for repetitive or strenuous tasks.
- **Posture and Balance Assessments:** Important for roles requiring coordination or long hours of standing or driving.

These evaluations simulate real job scenarios, allowing employers to make informed decisions and employees to understand their physical readiness before starting work.

- Specialized Screenings and Laboratory Tests

Depending on the position or employer's policy, additional tests may be included to assess overall health and prevent future workplace risks. These may include:

- Blood Work or Urinalysis for glucose, cholesterol, or kidney function
- EKG (Electrocardiogram) for cardiovascular screening
- Respiratory Function Testing for employees exposed to airborne hazards
- Immunization Verification (e.g., Hepatitis B, Tetanus, or COVID-19 vaccines)
- Tuberculosis (TB) or Infectious Disease Screening for healthcare workers

At WellGen Health, all testing adheres to HIPAA privacy regulations and industry best practices to protect your health information.
