Our multidisciplinary team includes experienced geriatricians, primary care physicians, nurses, dietitians, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and psychologists, all working collaboratively to deliver seamless, patient-centered care. We understand that aging affects more than the body; it influences lifestyle, independence, confidence, and emotional balance. That's why our comprehensive services are designed to promote vitality, resilience, and long-term well-being at every stage of life.

## Preventive screenings and early detection

Prevention is the cornerstone of longevity. At WellGen Health, we believe that early detection not only saves lives but also preserves independence and vitality. Our preventive programs focus on identifying risks before they develop into major health challenges, using cutting-edge diagnostics and age-specific screenings.

We offer a wide range of preventive tests and health evaluations that help detect early signs of disease, assess functional ability, and guide proactive treatment plans tailored to each patient's health profile.

- Cognitive & Memory Screening

Cognitive health is essential for independence and quality of life. Our geriatric specialists conduct detailed assessments to identify early indicators of Alzheimer's disease, dementia, and age-related memory loss. We then create customized brain health programs combining cognitive exercises, nutritional support, and lifestyle modifications to maintain mental sharpness and delay cognitive decline.

- Bone & Fall Risk Assessment

Falls are among the leading causes of injury in older adults. Through DEXA bone density scans and mobility evaluations, we assess bone strength, muscle balance, and fall risk. Based on findings, we design personalized fall-prevention and balance programs that include strength training, home safety recommendations, and flexibility exercises to enhance stability and reduce fracture risk.

- Cardio-Metabolic Evaluation

Heart and metabolic health are key pillars of longevity. Our comprehensive cardio-metabolic evaluations include cholesterol panels, blood pressure monitoring, glucose testing, and renal function assessments. These tests allow our specialists to detect cardiovascular or metabolic abnormalities early, ensuring timely interventions that support optimal heart, kidney, and vascular health.

- Cancer Screenings

Age increases the risk of several cancers, but early detection dramatically improves outcomes. WellGen Health provides age-appropriate cancer screenings following national and global guidelines. These include regular testing for prostate, breast, colorectal, and skin cancers; each supported by individualized follow-up plans and preventive counseling to promote long-term well-being.

- Medication Review

Many seniors manage multiple prescriptions, which can lead to medication overlaps, side effects, or drug interactions. Our comprehensive medication review process ensures every prescription aligns with the patient's health goals and medical history. By managing polypharmacy carefully, we enhance treatment outcomes, reduce risks, and improve quality of life.

## Chronic disease & condition management

Chronic diseases often become more prevalent with age, requiring continuous attention and coordinated care. At WellGen Health, we provide evidence-based chronic disease management programs that focus on stabilization, symptom relief, and lifestyle optimization.

Our goal is to empower older adults to manage their health conditions effectively while maintaining independence and vitality.

- Diabetes & Metabolic Disorders

We offer specialized programs for diabetes management and metabolic wellness, emphasizing nutrition therapy, glucose monitoring, and patient education. Our endocrinologists and dietitians work together to design individualized lifestyle and diet plans that support stable blood sugar, reduce complications, and promote energy balance.

- Cardiovascular Health

Our cardiology and geriatric teams collaborate to manage conditions such as hypertension, arrhythmias, coronary artery disease, and congestive heart failure. Through advanced diagnostics, medication optimization, and rehabilitation programs, we aim to strengthen cardiac function and prevent further complications.

- Arthritis & Musculoskeletal Health

Joint pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility are common aging concerns. WellGen Health provides non-surgical arthritis management, pain relief therapies, and mobility enhancement programs that combine physiotherapy, lifestyle interventions, and regenerative medicine to improve daily comfort and function.

- Pulmonary & Respiratory Care

For seniors with COPD, asthma, or chronic bronchitis, we deliver respiratory care programs focused on lung function improvement, medication optimization, and endurance training. Our goal is to enhance oxygen efficiency, reduce symptoms, and improve breathing capacity for a more active lifestyle.

- Renal & Urologic Health

Healthy kidney and urinary function are vital for overall wellness. Our specialists perform comprehensive renal evaluations, including eGFR and creatinine testing, to identify early kidney disease. We also provide hydration counseling, urinary tract management, and support for conditions like incontinence, ensuring comfort and dignity in everyday life.

## Wellness, rehabilitation & cognitive care

Healthy aging is not only about treating illness; it's about optimizing physical strength, emotional balance, and cognitive vitality. Our wellness and rehabilitation services combine traditional medicine with holistic approaches to nurture mind, body, and spirit.

## Personalized exercise programs

Our certified physiotherapists create customized exercise plans to improve strength, flexibility, and endurance. These programs enhance mobility, reduce pain, and prevent falls while supporting cardiovascular and musculoskeletal health.

- Nutritional Guidance

Proper nutrition is the foundation of healthy aging. Our dietitians design heart-healthy and brain-boosting diets tailored to seniors' changing metabolic needs. These plans help maintain ideal body weight, improve energy, and support organ function.

- Mental Health & Emotional Support

Emotional wellness plays a central role in longevity. Our mental health specialists offer counseling, mindfulness training, and group therapy to help seniors manage anxiety, depression, and adjustment to life transitions. We also provide family therapy and caregiver counseling to ensure comprehensive emotional support.

- Cognitive Fitness Programs

Brain health thrives on stimulation. Our neurocognitive training programs use targeted exercises, memory games, and problem-solving activities to improve concentration, reasoning, and learning capacity. These initiatives help delay cognitive decline and enhance mental resilience.

- Sleep and Stress Management

Restful sleep and emotional balance are essential for healthy aging. We provide sleep hygiene education, relaxation therapies, and stress reduction programs that help improve mood, reduce fatigue, and restore energy levels.

- Support for Independent Living

At WellGen Health, our mission extends beyond medical treatment; we strive to help seniors live independently and confidently in their homes and communities.

- Fall Prevention & Home Safety Assessments

Our geriatric experts assess home environments to identify potential hazards, providing personalized recommendations to improve safety and accessibility. Through adaptive equipment and balance training, we reduce fall risks and enhance mobility at home.

- Post-Hospital Rehabilitation

Transitioning from hospital to home can be challenging for older adults recovering from surgery, stroke, or illness. Our post-acute rehabilitation programs focus on rebuilding strength, restoring function, and ensuring a smooth recovery through coordinated therapy and follow-up care.

- Family & Caregiver Support

We recognize the vital role caregivers play in senior wellness. Our programs include education, emotional support, and resources to guide caregivers in managing daily routines, medications, and emotional challenges with confidence and compassion.

- Community Engagement

Isolation can affect both physical and emotional health. That's why WellGen Health encourages community participation through wellness workshops, senior fitness classes, and social events. These programs foster connection, purpose, and belonging among older adults.

Through our Comprehensive Geriatric Services, we help older adults live each day with confidence, dignity, and purpose; because at WellGen Health, we believe that the journey of aging should be a celebration of strength, wisdom, and well-being.
