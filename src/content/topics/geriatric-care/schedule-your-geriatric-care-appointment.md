## What to expect from your geriatric care appointment?

When you schedule an appointment with WellGen Health, you gain access to a comprehensive, patient-focused evaluation that goes beyond standard medical checkups. Our goal is to understand your complete health picture and develop a proactive plan that preserves independence and improves quality of life.

## 1. comprehensive geriatric health assessment

Your initial consultation begins with a thorough health evaluation covering physical, cognitive, emotional, and functional wellness. We review your medical history, current medications, nutrition habits, physical activity level, and mental health. This assessment helps us identify early signs of disease, prevent complications, and craft a personalized health roadmap.

Our geriatric health evaluations may include:

- **Physical health screening:** blood pressure, cholesterol, glucose, and bone density testing.
- **Cognitive and memory assessment:** identifying early signs of dementia or cognitive decline.
- **Emotional and psychological evaluation:** measuring mood, stress, and emotional resilience.
- **Mobility and fall risk testing:** assessing balance, strength, and flexibility.

This 360° approach ensures that every element of your well-being is considered; because true health extends beyond what's seen in lab results.

## 2. personalized care roadmap

At WellGen Health, we know that no two patients age the same way. That's why we design individualized care plans that reflect your lifestyle, personal goals, and medical priorities. Whether you want to improve heart health, maintain mental sharpness, manage arthritis, or stay active longer, your plan is custom-built to help you thrive.

Your personalized geriatric care roadmap may include:

- Nutrition counseling focused on energy, immunity, and brain health.
- Exercise and physiotherapy routines designed to maintain strength and balance.
- Medication reviews to ensure safety and minimize side effects.
- Cognitive enhancement and memory preservation strategies.
- Stress, sleep, and emotional wellness coaching.

We also provide digital tracking tools and telehealth support, making it easy to monitor your progress and communicate with your healthcare team from the comfort of your home.

## 3. ongoing support and preventive monitoring

Aging well is an ongoing journey, not a one-time effort. After your initial assessment, WellGen Health continues to partner with you through regular follow-ups, preventive screenings, and progress reviews. Our care team stays actively involved, adjusting your treatment plan as your needs evolve and ensuring that your health remains on track.

With advanced diagnostic technology and remote monitoring, we're able to detect even the smallest changes in your cardiovascular, renal, or cognitive health early; allowing timely interventions that preserve independence and vitality.
