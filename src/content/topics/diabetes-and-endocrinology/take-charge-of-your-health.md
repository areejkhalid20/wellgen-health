## Your first visit

Care starts with a full assessment that goes further than a routine check-up. Your endocrinologist looks at your hormones, metabolism, blood sugar control and daily habits. Tests such as blood sugar checks, hormone levels and metabolic screening help find the root cause of your symptoms, whether that's diabetes, a thyroid problem, an adrenal imbalance or a reproductive hormone issue.

## A plan made for you

Every body responds differently to treatment, food and stress, so there's no one-size-fits-all plan. Yours can include:

- **Medication:** insulin, tablets or hormone therapy matched to your results.
- **Nutrition therapy:** an eating plan from a dietitian that keeps blood sugar and hormones steady.
- **Exercise:** safe activity you enjoy, to improve insulin sensitivity, heart health and energy.
- **Stress support:** mindfulness, breathing exercises and counseling to lower stress hormones.

We track your progress and adjust your plan based on your results.

## Conditions we treat

- Diabetes: type 1, type 2 and gestational
- Thyroid conditions, including Hashimoto's disease
- Adrenal disorders, including Cushing's syndrome and Addison's disease
- Pituitary conditions affecting growth and reproductive hormones
- PCOS and related fertility concerns
- Osteoporosis and calcium disorders

## Knowing what to do and why

You'll learn how hormones, food and daily habits affect each other, how to check your blood sugar, what warning signs to look for and how to manage your medication. Online tools and apps let you track your glucose, meals and exercise and message your care team.

## Care that continues

Your care doesn't stop after diagnosis. Continuous glucose monitoring and regular check-ins with your endocrinologist, dietitian and health coach let us adjust your treatment early and keep you on track.

## Prevention for the long term

Regular screenings and risk checks help us catch problems early, from diabetes complications to heart and bone health. Because living with a long-term condition can affect your mood, emotional support is part of every care plan.
