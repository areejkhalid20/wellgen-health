Hormones act as your body's internal communication system, regulating nearly every biological function; from how well you sleep and think to how efficiently you burn calories and respond to stress. When hormone levels decline or fluctuate, the effects can be wide-ranging, including fatigue, irritability, weight gain, low libido, and decreased motivation.
