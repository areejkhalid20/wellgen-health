## Understanding hormones and their role in the body

Hormones are chemical messengers produced by glands in the endocrine system, including the thyroid, adrenal glands, ovaries, and testes. They circulate through the bloodstream, controlling nearly every bodily process; from metabolism and reproduction to mood regulation and immune function. When hormone levels are optimal, your body operates like a finely tuned system. But when production declines or becomes imbalanced, even small shifts can lead to significant health changes.

For women, this decline is often most noticeable during perimenopause and menopause, when levels of estrogen and progesterone drop sharply. For men, hormonal decline tends to occur more gradually in a process called andropause, marked by reduced testosterone levels. Both experiences can lead to fatigue, mood changes, weight gain, sexual dysfunction, and cognitive difficulties.

Hormone Replacement Therapy aims to restore these hormones to their optimal levels; helping individuals regain vitality, stability, and overall well-being.

- Hot flashes and night sweats
- Mood swings and irritability
- Weight gain and slowed metabolism
- Vaginal dryness or pain during intimacy
- Sleep disturbances and anxiety

Men, on the other hand, may experience low testosterone or andropause, which can lead to:

- Loss of muscle mass and strength
- Fatigue and low energy
