## Types of hormone replacement therapy

Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT)

One of the most advanced and natural forms of hormone therapy available today is Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT). Bioidentical hormones are derived from plant sources, such as yams or soy, and are molecularly identical to the hormones your body produces naturally. This allows for a more seamless integration with your body's biochemistry, resulting in fewer side effects and more stable results.

BHRT is commonly used to restore estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone in both men and women. Women benefit from BHRT in addressing menopausal symptoms such as hot flashes, night sweats, mood swings, and vaginal dryness. Men experience improved energy, muscle strength, and libido as testosterone levels return to normal.

At WellGen Health, we use advanced compounding techniques to create customized BHRT formulations tailored to your specific hormonal profile. This ensures precise dosing that aligns with your body's needs; nothing more, nothing less.

## Traditional or synthetic hormone therapy

In certain cases, synthetic hormone therapy may be recommended for targeted treatment of specific hormonal deficiencies or medical conditions. These therapies use FDA-approved medications that have been extensively studied and are effective for managing conditions like thyroid disorders, severe estrogen deficiency, or adrenal dysfunction.

Our experienced medical team evaluates whether bioidentical or traditional hormone therapy; or a combination of both; will yield the most effective outcome for your health and wellness goals. Our focus remains on safety, balance, and measurable improvement in your quality of life

## Delivery methods for hormone replacement therapy

Hormone Replacement Therapy can be administered in several convenient and effective forms, allowing flexibility for every lifestyle. At WellGen Health, we help you choose the most appropriate delivery method based on your preferences, absorption rate, and medical history.

Oral Tablets or Capsules

For patients who prefer a familiar and convenient option, oral hormone therapy provides steady hormone absorption through the digestive system. Estrogen, progesterone, and thyroid hormones are commonly prescribed in tablet or capsule form. While easy to use, oral therapy requires consistent dosing and periodic monitoring to ensure optimal hormone levels.

Topical Creams or Gels

Topical hormones are applied directly to the skin, where they are absorbed into the bloodstream. This method bypasses the digestive system, allowing for smoother, more consistent hormone delivery. Topical estrogen, progesterone, or testosterone creams and gels are especially beneficial for those who prefer gradual absorption and easy daily use.

Transdermal Patches

Skin patches are a convenient way to deliver hormones continuously over 24 to 48 hours. They offer stable hormone levels and reduce fluctuations that can occur with oral or injectable forms. Patches are often recommended for patients with sensitive digestive systems or those seeking a low-maintenance option.

Hormone Pellets

For long-term, hassle-free hormone therapy, pellet implantation is one of the most effective and popular options. Small, rice-sized pellets containing bioidentical hormones are inserted just beneath the skin; typically in the hip or buttock area; during a quick, in-office procedure. These pellets release a steady, consistent dose of hormones over 3 to 6 months, eliminating the need for daily medication.

Pellet therapy offers several advantages: no daily dosing, minimal fluctuations, and sustained energy and mood balance. At WellGen Health, our specialists ensure the insertion process is quick, comfortable, and tailored to your individual needs.

Hormone Injections

Hormone injections are ideal for patients seeking rapid results or who require precise control over dosage. Injectable testosterone, for example, is a common and highly effective treatment for men with low testosterone levels. Injections can restore strength, stamina, and libido within weeks, under the careful supervision of our medical team.

Each delivery method has unique benefits, and your WellGen Health provider will recommend the best option based on your hormone levels, goals, and lifestyle.
