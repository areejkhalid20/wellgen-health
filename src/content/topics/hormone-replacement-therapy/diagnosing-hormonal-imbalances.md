## Comprehensive hormone testing at WellGen Health

- Blood Testing for Key Hormone Levels

The cornerstone of hormonal evaluation is a complete blood hormone panel. This test measures the levels of essential hormones that regulate everything from reproductive health to metabolism and stress response.Our standard blood panel includes:

- Estrogen and Progesterone – to assess reproductive balance, menopause, or perimenopause symptoms in women.
- Testosterone (Total and Free) – to evaluate testosterone deficiency or andropause in men and sexual wellness in both genders.
- Thyroid Hormones (TSH, T3, T4) – to identify hypo- or hyperthyroidism, which can affect metabolism, weight, and mood.
- DHEA and Cortisol – to assess adrenal function and the body's stress response.
- Insulin and Glucose Levels – to screen for metabolic or insulin resistance that can interfere with hormonal balance.
- Vitamin D and Lipid Profile – because nutrient deficiencies can amplify hormonal symptoms.

Each result is analyzed in context with your medical history, ensuring that we uncover the why behind your imbalance; not just the what.

- Saliva and Urine Hormone Testing

While blood tests measure baseline hormone levels, saliva and urine testing provide insight into hormone activity throughout the day. Hormone production fluctuates over a 24-hour period, and these tests allow us to track patterns and cycles that might not be visible in a single blood draw.Saliva testing is particularly valuable for measuring free (active) hormones such as cortisol, estrogen, progesterone, and testosterone.Urine hormone testing provides a detailed picture of how your body metabolizes hormones, helping us evaluate detoxification pathways and potential imbalances in hormone breakdown.

By combining results from both methods, our clinicians obtain a comprehensive map of your hormonal rhythm; allowing for precise, personalized HRT dosing.

- Thyroid and Metabolic Function Testing

Your thyroid gland plays a critical role in regulating metabolism, temperature, energy, and cognitive function. Even minor thyroid imbalances can mimic symptoms of menopause, low testosterone, or adrenal fatigue. At WellGen Health, we go beyond basic thyroid screening by assessing:

- Free T3 and Free T4 – the active thyroid hormones that drive cellular metabolism.
- Reverse T3 – to detect whether your body is converting thyroid hormones properly.
- Thyroid Antibodies (TPO, TG) – to identify autoimmune conditions such as Hashimoto's thyroiditis.

We also evaluate metabolic indicators like fasting glucose, insulin, and lipid panels to determine if insulin resistance or metabolic syndrome is contributing to your hormonal disruption. By identifying these factors early, our specialists can address them before they evolve into chronic conditions.

- Adrenal Health and Cortisol Rhythm Testing

The adrenal glands are small but powerful hormone producers that control your body's stress response through cortisol and DHEA. Chronic stress, poor sleep, or overtraining can lead to adrenal dysfunction, resulting in fatigue, mood instability, and weight changes.Through salivary cortisol rhythm testing, we measure cortisol levels at four intervals throughout the day to assess your adrenal rhythm. If imbalances are found, our team develops strategies that may include nutritional support, stress management techniques, and targeted HRT when necessary.

- Body Composition and Vital Signs Analysis

Hormones directly influence how your body stores fat, builds muscle, and maintains energy. We use advanced body composition analysis to measure fat distribution, muscle mass, water retention, and bone density. Combined with vital signs monitoring; including blood pressure, heart rate, and temperature regulation; this data helps us understand how hormonal imbalance is impacting your physical health and metabolism.

- Lifestyle, Nutrition, and Stress Evaluation

Hormones don't exist in isolation; they respond to your environment, habits, and emotions. At WellGen Health, we take time to understand your daily lifestyle: sleep patterns, stress levels, exercise routines, and diet quality. These factors can influence hormone function as much as biological age or genetics.Through lifestyle questionnaires and functional assessments, we identify triggers such as chronic stress, nutrient deficiencies, and sleep deprivation that might be contributing to your imbalance. This comprehensive view allows us to create a truly individualized treatment plan.
