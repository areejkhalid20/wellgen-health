## Cardiovascular diagnostics

Cardiovascular diagnostic testing focuses on evaluating heart rhythm, blood flow, electrical activity, and structural function. At WellGen Health, we use advanced, non-invasive methods to reveal even the smallest abnormalities in heart performance or circulation.

## 1. electrocardiogram (ECG or EKG)

The electrocardiogram is one of the most essential tools in heart diagnostics. It records the electrical activity of the heart through sensors placed on the skin. Our ECG technology detects irregular heart rhythms (arrhythmias), evidence of past or current heart attacks, and abnormalities in heart rate variability that may suggest underlying cardiac stress.

We use both resting ECG for baseline analysis and ambulatory (Holter) ECG monitoring to capture electrical activity over 24–48 hours, ensuring transient or stress-induced irregularities are not missed.

## 2. echocardiogram

An echocardiogram uses high-frequency ultrasound waves to produce real-time images of the heart. This non-invasive test helps assess heart chamber size, valve function, and pumping strength (ejection fraction). It's invaluable in diagnosing heart failure, valvular diseases, congenital abnormalities, and cardiomyopathies.

At WellGen Health, our cardiac sonographers use 3D and Doppler echocardiography to evaluate blood flow direction, velocity, and pressure gradients; providing a detailed assessment of your heart's mechanical efficiency.

## 3. stress testing (exercise or pharmacologic)

To evaluate how your heart performs under physical exertion, our specialists conduct cardiac stress tests. During this test, you walk or jog on a treadmill (or receive a medication that simulates exercise) while your heart rate, blood pressure, and ECG readings are monitored.

Stress testing helps identify coronary artery blockages, exercise intolerance, and hidden arrhythmias that may not appear when the body is at rest. It also assists in determining the safety of exercise programs for patients recovering from cardiac events.

## 4. advanced cardiac imaging: CT, MRI, and angiography

Modern imaging technology allows us to visualize the heart and vascular structures in unprecedented detail.

- Cardiac CT scans reveal calcified plaque buildup and assess coronary artery disease risk.
- Cardiac MRI provides high-resolution images of soft tissue, helping detect heart muscle damage, inflammation, and structural defects.
- Coronary angiography (non-invasive CT or catheter-based) maps blood flow through coronary arteries, identifying blockages or narrowing that could lead to heart attacks.

WellGen Health uses low-radiation, high-precision imaging protocols to ensure safety and accuracy, delivering detailed insights into cardiac anatomy and function.

## 5. lipid and blood pressure monitoring

Even before symptoms arise, elevated cholesterol and high blood pressure can silently damage arteries and heart tissue. Continuous blood pressure monitoring, advanced lipid panels, and metabolic profiling form part of our diagnostic program. These screenings help identify risk factors for atherosclerosis, stroke, and heart failure; allowing timely lifestyle or therapeutic intervention.

By tracking key biomarkers such as LDL, HDL, triglycerides, and inflammatory markers, we can personalize prevention plans for long-term heart protection.

## Pulmonary diagnostics

Pulmonary diagnostics at WellGen Health focus on airflow dynamics, oxygen efficiency, and lung tissue integrity. Our comprehensive respiratory testing allows clinicians to detect both acute and chronic lung disorders with precision, ensuring that patients receive accurate diagnoses and effective, customized treatment plans.

## 1. pulmonary function testing (PFT)

Pulmonary Function Tests are foundational to assessing lung capacity and performance. These tests measure total lung volume, airflow speed, and gas exchange efficiency, revealing early signs of conditions such as asthma, COPD, restrictive lung disease, or pulmonary fibrosis.

Our advanced PFT systems provide detailed feedback on lung elasticity, airway resistance, and diffusion capacity, helping to guide ongoing respiratory therapy or rehabilitation.

## 2. spirometry and diffusion tests

Spirometry is one of the most common pulmonary tests, measuring how much air you can inhale and exhale, and how quickly you can do it. It helps diagnose obstructive diseases (like COPD or asthma) and restrictive conditions (like interstitial lung disease).

Diffusion capacity tests (DLCO) evaluate how efficiently oxygen passes from the lungs into the bloodstream, identifying problems in the alveoli or capillaries. At WellGen Health, these tests are performed using advanced digital spirometers for accurate, reproducible results.

## 3. chest x-rays and high-resolution CT scans

Imaging plays a pivotal role in diagnosing pulmonary disorders. Chest X-rays provide a quick overview of lung health; revealing signs of infection, fluid buildup, or structural changes. For more detailed evaluation, high-resolution CT (HRCT) scans detect early signs of fibrosis, nodules, tumors, or chronic inflammatory changes invisible on X-rays.

Our radiologists use AI-enhanced imaging systems to ensure every abnormality, however subtle, is identified and evaluated with precision.

## 4. oxygen saturation studies and arterial blood gas (ABG) analysis

Maintaining adequate oxygenation is vital to life. Pulse oximetry and arterial blood gas analysis measure how effectively oxygen is being delivered to your tissues and how efficiently carbon dioxide is removed. These tests are crucial in assessing respiratory efficiency, especially in patients with COPD, sleep apnea, or heart failure.

At WellGen Health, continuous oxygen saturation monitoring is available for both in-clinic and home-based care programs, supporting ongoing management of chronic respiratory disorders.

## 5. allergy and immunologic testing

Allergic reactions are a frequent cause of respiratory distress. Through comprehensive allergy panels, skin-prick testing, and immunologic evaluations, we identify environmental, occupational, or food allergens that trigger or worsen symptoms of asthma, rhinitis, or chronic bronchitis.

Pinpointing these triggers allows our specialists to design personalized avoidance and desensitization strategies, significantly improving respiratory comfort and long-term lung function.

## Integrating cardiac and pulmonary diagnostics

What sets WellGen Health apart is our integrated cardio-pulmonary diagnostic model. Rather than treating the heart and lungs as separate entities, our specialists assess how they interact; because conditions like heart failure, pulmonary hypertension, or sleep apnea often involve both systems.

This collaborative model ensures that a symptom such as shortness of breath isn't evaluated in isolation but in the context of both cardiovascular and pulmonary performance. With this approach, we can distinguish between heart-related breathlessness and lung-related limitations, ensuring precise treatment that addresses the true underlying cause.
