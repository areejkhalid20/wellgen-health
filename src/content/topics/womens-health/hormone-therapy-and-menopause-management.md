## Understanding menopause and hormonal changes

Menopause is a natural biological process marking the end of a woman's reproductive years, typically occurring between ages 45 and 55. The years leading up to it; known as perimenopause; can last several years and are characterized by fluctuating hormone levels. During this time, many women begin to notice changes such as:

- Irregular menstrual cycles
- Hot flashes and night sweats
- Mood swings and irritability
- Fatigue and sleep disturbances
- Weight gain or metabolic shifts
- Vaginal dryness and changes in sexual health
- Decreased bone density and muscle mass
- Reduced focus or memory changes

These symptoms vary in intensity for each woman and may last for months or years. At WellGen Health, our menopause specialists take time to understand your body's unique hormonal patterns, helping you find safe, effective, and lasting relief.

## Personalized hormone therapy plans

Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) remains one of the most effective treatments for alleviating moderate to severe menopause symptoms caused by hormonal decline. At WellGen Health, we offer individualized hormone therapy tailored to your medical history, lifestyle, and comfort level. Our goal is to restore hormonal balance, relieve disruptive symptoms, and protect long-term health.

We provide several hormone therapy options, including:

- **Systemic Hormone Therapy:** Estrogen or combined estrogen-progesterone treatments delivered via oral tablets, patches, gels, or injections.
- **Local Hormone Therapy:** Low-dose vaginal creams, rings, or tablets targeting dryness and discomfort without significant systemic absorption.
- **Bioidentical Hormone Replacement Therapy (BHRT):** Customized, plant-based hormone formulations that mimic the body's natural hormones, prescribed when appropriate and monitored closely.

## Managing perimenopause with confidence

Perimenopause; the transition before menopause; often begins several years prior to a woman's final period. This stage can bring unpredictable changes in menstrual patterns, mood, and energy levels. Many women find perimenopause to be one of the most challenging times hormonally.

At WellGen Health, we help women identify early signs of hormonal imbalance and manage them effectively. Regular check-ups, hormone testing, and lifestyle interventions can ease symptoms and reduce risks associated with later menopause stages. Addressing perimenopausal health early helps prevent future complications and ensures a smoother transition into menopause.
