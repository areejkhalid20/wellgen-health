- Have mobility limitations or chronic conditions that make travel challenging
- Maintain busy work schedules or family responsibilities
- Prefer to receive medical guidance from the privacy of home

For many patients, telemedicine isn't just convenient; it's transformative. It empowers them to take control of their health, fostering consistency, accountability, and better long-term outcomes.
