## A step-by-step guide to your virtual care experience

Our telemedicine system is built for ease of use, even if you're not tech-savvy. From scheduling your appointment to receiving your care plan, every step is straightforward, private, and efficient.

Here's exactly how telemedicine works at WellGen Health:

- Schedule Your Appointment with Ease

Booking a telemedicine visit is quick and simple. You can schedule an appointment online through our secure patient portal or call our office directly to select a convenient time. Once your appointment is confirmed, you'll receive an email or text message with your personalized, encrypted link for your virtual session.

Our scheduling system is flexible; allowing you to choose appointment times that fit your routine, whether it's early morning, during your lunch break, or after work hours.

For patients across Texas, this step eliminates the challenges of distance, traffic, and tight schedules, making healthcare truly accessible from Dallas to Houston, Austin to San Antonio, and everywhere in between.

- Prepare for Your Virtual Session

Before your telemedicine appointment, take a few minutes to gather relevant information that will help your provider understand your health situation. This may include:

- A list of your current medications and dosages
- Recent lab results or diagnostic reports
- Notes about new symptoms, concerns, or lifestyle changes
- Your vital readings, such as blood pressure or glucose levels (if applicable)

It's also a good idea to test your internet connection and ensure your camera and microphone are working properly. You can join your session using a smartphone, tablet, or computer; whichever device feels most comfortable.

This brief preparation helps your provider deliver a more accurate and personalized consultation while saving time during your visit.

- Connect Securely on Our HIPAA-Compliant Platform

When it's time for your appointment, simply click the link provided in your confirmation message. You'll be automatically connected to a secure, encrypted video session through WellGen Health's HIPAA-compliant telemedicine platform.

Your privacy and confidentiality are our top priorities. All video, audio, and data transmissions are protected under strict healthcare security standards, ensuring that your medical information remains safe and confidential.

Our user-friendly system requires no software downloads or technical setup; you can join the session directly from your browser. If you experience any issues, our support team is available to guide you through the connection process, ensuring a smooth and worry-free experience.

- Consult with Your WellGen Health Provider

Once connected, your telemedicine consultation begins; just as it would during an in-person visit. Your provider will greet you, review your medical history, and discuss your concerns in detail.

During this one-on-one video session, you can:

- Describe your symptoms, lifestyle, or ongoing health challenges
- Show visible concerns (such as rashes, swelling, or injuries) via camera
- Discuss medication side effects or adjustments
- Review your progress for chronic conditions like diabetes, hypertension, or asthma
- Ask questions about lab results, prescriptions, or wellness strategies

Your provider may conduct a visual assessment and, based on your discussion, offer a diagnosis, treatment plan, or medication prescription if appropriate. In some cases, you may be asked to schedule a follow-up telemedicine visit or an in-person appointment for further evaluation.

Throughout the process, our providers maintain a compassionate, conversational approach; ensuring you feel heard, understood, and supported every step of the way.

- Receive Your Personalized Care Plan

After your virtual visit, you'll receive a detailed summary of your consultation, which may include:

- A diagnosis or assessment of your condition
- Recommended treatment options or medication
- Lifestyle and nutritional guidance (if applicable)
- Follow-up care instructions and recommended appointment timelines

If medications are prescribed, they are sent electronically to your preferred pharmacy; so you can conveniently pick them up without delay.

You'll also have access to your provider's notes and any recommended resources through our secure patient portal, allowing you to review the information anytime.

Should you need additional support or a follow-up consultation, scheduling is simple; just log in, select your provider, and book your next telemedicine session.

## Beyond virtual visits: an extension of comprehensive care

Telemedicine at WellGen Health isn't merely a digital replacement for traditional office visits; it's a seamless extension of our holistic approach to healthcare.

Our virtual consultations integrate directly with your existing care plan, ensuring that your provider has full access to your medical history and ongoing treatments. This continuity allows us to make better-informed decisions and provide truly personalized care.

Telemedicine is ideal for:

- Follow-up visits after in-person exams or procedures

- Medication reviews and prescription renewals
- Lifestyle counseling, including nutrition and weight management
- Preventive care and wellness check-ins

Our goal is to ensure that your healthcare experience remains consistent, coordinated, and accessible; whether you visit us virtually or in person.
