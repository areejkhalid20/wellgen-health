## Understanding the root causes of hypertension

High blood pressure develops when the balance between blood flow, arterial resistance, and fluid regulation in the body becomes disrupted. The heart pumps blood with a certain force, and the arteries resist that force to maintain stability. When arteries become narrower, stiffer, or overloaded with volume due to salt retention or hormonal imbalances, the heart must work harder to push blood through.

This ongoing pressure causes micro-damage to artery walls, triggering inflammation and plaque buildup that further reduces elasticity. The process is gradual but progressive; setting the stage for atherosclerosis, heart strain, and kidney injury.

There are two major categories of hypertension, each influenced by different causes and risk factors:

- **Primary (Essential) Hypertension:** This is the most common type, accounting for nearly 90–95% of all cases. It develops gradually over many years without a specific underlying disease but is strongly linked to lifestyle habits, genetic tendencies, and age-related vascular changes.
- **Secondary Hypertension:** This form is driven by an identifiable cause such as kidney disease, hormonal disorders, sleep apnea, or certain medications. It often appears suddenly and may be more difficult to control until the root problem is addressed.

At WellGen Health, our specialists carefully distinguish between these two forms using advanced diagnostics, ensuring that your care plan addresses the true cause, not just the symptoms.
