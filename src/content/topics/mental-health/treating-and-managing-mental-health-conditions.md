## Professional counseling & psychotherapy

Psychotherapy lies at the heart of our mental health care philosophy. It's a process of healing through connection, reflection, and transformation. Our licensed psychologists and therapists offer individual, group, and family therapy sessions that encourage self-awareness, healthy coping, and emotional renewal.

Individual Therapy

In one-on-one sessions, patients are guided to explore their thoughts, emotions, and experiences in a safe, nonjudgmental environment. Individual therapy helps with:

- Depression and low mood
- Anxiety and panic attacks
- Trauma recovery and PTSD
- Self-esteem and identity concerns
- Stress management and life transitions

Our therapists utilize evidence-based modalities such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), and Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT). These techniques promote emotional regulation, build coping skills, and encourage positive behavioral change.

## Group therapy

Humans heal best in connection with others. Group therapy at WellGen Health provides a supportive environment for individuals to share experiences, learn from peers, and practice interpersonal skills. Led by clinical professionals, our group sessions address topics like stress reduction, grief processing, social anxiety, and emotional resilience.

## Family & couples counseling

Mental health affects not just individuals but also families and relationships. Our therapists work with couples and families to enhance communication, resolve conflict, and rebuild trust. Whether managing a loved one's depression or navigating marital challenges, our family therapy sessions promote understanding, compassion, and lasting harmony.

## Psychiatric medication management

For some conditions, psychotherapy alone may not be enough. When medication is indicated, our board-certified psychiatrists design treatment plans that integrate pharmacological therapy with counseling and lifestyle support.

We prescribe and monitor medications with utmost precision and care, focusing on safety, minimal side effects, and maximum therapeutic benefit. Our medication management program is ideal for conditions such as:

- Major Depressive Disorder
- Bipolar Disorder
- Generalized Anxiety and Panic Disorders
- Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
- Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
- Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

Our psychiatrists use the latest advancements in psychopharmacology and adhere to evidence-based international guidelines. Every treatment plan is closely monitored through follow-up appointments, digital health tracking, and ongoing patient feedback.

## Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)

CBT is one of the most widely researched and effective therapies in modern psychology. At WellGen Health, CBT forms a cornerstone of many personalized care plans.

Through structured sessions, patients learn to identify distorted thinking patterns, challenge self-defeating beliefs, and replace them with constructive thought processes. CBT helps individuals:

- Overcome negative self-talk and pessimism
- Manage anxiety and panic by reframing fear responses
- Develop healthy coping mechanisms for stress
- Break cycles of avoidance, guilt, or perfectionism
- Regain motivation and a sense of control over their lives

Our CBT specialists tailor each session to the individual's pace, ensuring practical, goal-oriented, and lasting transformation.
