## The importance of proper diagnosis

Obesity is far more than a cosmetic concern; it is a chronic, multifactorial disease that affects nearly every system of the body. Without accurate diagnosis, even the most disciplined diet or exercise plan may fail to deliver lasting results. That's why WellGen Health's diagnostic protocols focus on identifying not just how much weight you carry, but why your body stores and regulates fat the way it does.

Through advanced medical evaluation, we assess how your body processes calories, how your hormones influence hunger and metabolism, and how genetic or lifestyle factors may contribute to weight gain. This approach allows our specialists to pinpoint issues such as insulin resistance, thyroid dysfunction, hormonal imbalances, or metabolic slowdown that often go undetected in routine check-ups.

## Body mass index (BMI): the first step

One of the most widely used indicators of obesity is the Body Mass Index (BMI); a calculation that compares your weight to your height to estimate overall body fat. Although BMI is only one aspect of diagnosis, it serves as a valuable screening tool to categorize weight status and potential health risks.

BMI Classifications:

- **Normal Weight:** 18.5–24.9
- **Overweight:** 25–29.9
- **Obese (Class I):** 30–34.9
- **Severely Obese (Class II):** 35–39.9
- **Morbidly Obese (Class III):** 40 or higher

While BMI offers a useful starting point, it does not account for differences in muscle mass, bone density, or fat distribution. Two people may have identical BMIs but very different metabolic health profiles. That's why WellGen Health goes beyond BMI to provide a multi-dimensional obesity evaluation.

## Body composition analysis

To gain deeper insight into how your body stores fat and builds muscle, we conduct a comprehensive body composition analysis. This advanced testing measures the exact percentage of body fat, lean muscle mass, and visceral fat; the fat stored around internal organs that poses the greatest health risks.

Our diagnostic tools help determine:

- **Total Body Fat Percentage:** Identifying excess fat relative to healthy ranges for your age and gender.
- **Visceral Fat Index:** Assessing internal fat linked to metabolic diseases like diabetes and heart disease.
- **Muscle Mass and Bone Density:** Evaluating strength, metabolism efficiency, and overall body composition balance.

Understanding these ratios helps us develop targeted weight loss plans that prioritize fat reduction without sacrificing muscle tone or metabolic function. At WellGen Health, our goal is to promote healthy body recompositing; reducing harmful fat while building sustainable strength and endurance.

## Waist circumference and waist-to-hip ratio

Central obesity; or fat accumulation around the abdomen; is one of the strongest predictors of cardiovascular disease and metabolic syndrome. To measure this risk accurately, our specialists assess both waist circumference and waist-to-hip ratio (WHR).

- **Waist Circumference:** A measurement above 40 inches for men or 35 inches for women indicates a higher risk of heart disease and diabetes.
- **Waist-to-Hip Ratio:** Calculated by dividing waist measurement by hip measurement, it evaluates fat distribution patterns. A WHR above 0.90 in men or 0.85 in women suggests increased visceral fat.

These simple yet powerful metrics provide valuable insight into abdominal fat accumulation; a key component of obesity-related complications. Combined with other diagnostic results, they allow our team to personalize exercise and nutrition strategies that specifically target central fat reduction.

## Laboratory and metabolic testing

At WellGen Health, we perform extensive laboratory tests to evaluate the biochemical and hormonal factors influencing your weight. These assessments reveal how efficiently your body metabolizes nutrients and whether any imbalances are hindering fat loss.

Our laboratory evaluations typically include:

- **Fasting Blood Glucose and HbA1c:** To screen for prediabetes or diabetes.
- **Lipid Profile:** Measuring cholesterol and triglyceride levels to assess heart health.
- **Thyroid Function Tests (TSH, T3, T4):** Detecting thyroid disorders that may slow metabolism.
- **Hormonal Panel:** Evaluating levels of cortisol, estrogen, testosterone, leptin, and insulin that influence appetite and fat storage.
- **Liver and Kidney Function Tests:** Ensuring organs are functioning properly to support healthy metabolism.

In addition, we may conduct metabolic rate testing to determine your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR); the number of calories your body burns at rest. Understanding your unique metabolism helps us tailor dietary and exercise recommendations to maximize fat loss and preserve lean muscle mass.
