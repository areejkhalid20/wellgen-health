## Core specialization and clinical focus

As a leading Kidney Specialist and Board-Certified Internist, Dr. Memon offers comprehensive, expert care for a wide spectrum of renal and systemic conditions. His key areas of expertise include:

- **Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management:** Expert care for all stages of Chronic Kidney Disease, focusing on slowing progression and improving patient quality of life.
- **Hypertension (High Blood Pressure):** Specialized diagnosis and management of resistant hypertension, particularly when it relates to kidney function.
- **Electrolyte & Acid-Base Imbalances:** Precise management of complex fluid and electrolyte disorders, including imbalances of sodium, potassium, and calcium.
- **Acute Renal Failure (Acute Kidney Injury – AKI):** Rapid diagnosis and critical care management of sudden acute kidney failure.
- **Renal Replacement Therapy:** Expertise in all modalities of Dialysis (Hemodialysis and Peritoneal Dialysis) and Kidney Transplant evaluation and follow-up.

## Professional background and affiliations

Dr. Memon is a graduate of Dow Medical College in Karachi. He completed his Internal Medicine residency at William Beaumont Hospital in Royal Oak, Michigan, followed by specialized Nephrology fellowship training at Washington University, St. Louis, Missouri.

He maintains extensive hospital affiliations throughout the DFW metroplex, including:

- Medical City Fort Worth
- Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Dallas and multiple other Texas Health Resources facilities (Texas Health Allen, Alliance, Arlington Memorial, etc.)

Dr. Memon practices with Tarrant Nephrology Associates in Fort Worth, TX, where he and his colleagues are committed to excellence in all aspects of kidney care.

## Patient philosophy

Dr. Memon's care philosophy emphasizes a transparent and evidence-based approach. He ensures patients are fully informed and involved in their treatment plan, providing the highest standard of care while focusing on effective communication and addressing all aspects of their well-being.
