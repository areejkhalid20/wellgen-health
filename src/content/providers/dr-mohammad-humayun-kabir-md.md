## Expertise in advanced kidney care

Dr. Kabir is skilled in managing complex renal and metabolic conditions, focusing on long-term patient well-being and preventive strategies. His key areas of expertise include:

- **Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Management:** Expert care across all stages of CKD, with a focus on progression slowing and preservation of kidney function.
- **Hypertension & Renal Health:** Specialized diagnosis and treatment of high blood pressure, particularly cases related to or impacting the kidneys.
- **Electrolyte & Fluid Balance:** Management of complex electrolyte imbalances and fluid overload conditions.
- **Acute Renal Failure (AKI):** Timely diagnosis and management of acute kidney injury.
- **Kidney Stone Prevention:** Developing comprehensive medical strategies to prevent the recurrence of kidney stones.
- **Renal Replacement Therapy:** Involvement in patient care related to dialysis (Hemodialysis, Peritoneal Dialysis) and Kidney Transplant evaluation.

## Professional background and affiliations

Dr. Kabir earned his medical degree from Mymensingh Medical College (1997). He completed his rigorous Internal Medicine residency and subsequent Nephrology fellowship training at the University of Kansas School of Medicine.

He maintains extensive hospital affiliations across North Texas, including:

- Baylor Scott & White Health (e.g., Medical Center – Grapevine, All Saints Medical Center – Fort Worth)
- Texas Health Resources (e.g., Texas Health HEB, Texas Health Dallas)

Dr. Kabir practices with Texas Kidney Associates and PPG Health, PA, offering his specialized services across multiple clinic locations in areas like Bedford and Irving, TX.
